Take 

[SRR1051536](https://trace.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Traces/sra/?run=SRR1051536)

 as an example 

1\. go visit https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra/SRX393264 
2\. click on the SRR1051536 to go to the sra site 
3\. find the experiment ID [SRX393264](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra/SRX393264), copy it 
4\. move cursor over the "Download" on the  upper panel  and click "FASTA/FASTQ" 
5\. paste the experiment ID, click "show runs" 
6\. choose "FASTQ" as the format of data you want to download, click the "download" on the bottom right.
