To protect your clothes not frequently worn from getting dust accumulated on the collar/shoulders, you need something called a travel cover, which is available on Amazon or in laundry shops. 

But it can also be free. 

1\. Get a big clean plastic bag. i.e. a big plastic bag from Ralph's grocery supermarket 
2\. The Bag should be a Rectangle-shaped thingy with one side open and three sides connected, like an U. Cut open one side so it becomes L 
3\. make a hole at the uncut corner so that L becomes |\_ 
4\. rotate this. The hole is for your clothes-hanger's top; the two open sides are where your clothes come in.
