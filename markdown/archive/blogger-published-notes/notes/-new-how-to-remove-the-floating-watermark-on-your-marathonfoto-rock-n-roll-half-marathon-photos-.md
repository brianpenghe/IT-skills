Update Nov 1: 
Since their website changed the code, my previous Method 2 and Method 3 won't work. 
Let me update the method now: 
Method 3: Open the webpage and your photo using Google chrome, press F12, search keyword "render.mara" by Control+F  , click on the command line you found in the bottom left window, then the link will show up in the bottom right window. Right click, then select "open the link in new tab" 

\~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~previous methods~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 
First open the page and enlarge the photo you like 
**Method 1:** 
**Take 10 consecutive screen shots, and open these 10 photos using ImageJ, Take the median value of these 10 photos.** 

Method 2: 
Save the page to your local directory, open the saved page using chrome, press F12, control+F to search for the keyword "watermark", delete that command line. 

Method 3: 
Save the page to your local directory, press F12, Control+F to search the keyword "render.mara". In this line you will see a link to a new page. Open that page, you will have removed the moving watermark.
