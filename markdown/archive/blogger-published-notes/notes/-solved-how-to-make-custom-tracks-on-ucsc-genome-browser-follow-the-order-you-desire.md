When uploading multiple groups of customer tracks, you may find that UCSC merge your lists so that tracks in Group B don't necessarily appear below tracks in Group A. 

In that case you can do this to make sure the two groups of tracks are separated: 

Upload tracks in Group A first, 
**and then, go to the browser to view it,** 
and then manage custom tracks -> add Group B tracks. 

In that way Group B will be below Group A
