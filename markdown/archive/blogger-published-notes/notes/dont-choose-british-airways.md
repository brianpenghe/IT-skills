That totally destroyed my following flights and meetings.

2\. We were also inappropriately treated after that. We were made to wait in a long long line among other angry passengers who were also delayed and upset. 4\. Therefore, my second flight was also missed because of that. And I had to stay in Las Vegas for a horrifying night.
