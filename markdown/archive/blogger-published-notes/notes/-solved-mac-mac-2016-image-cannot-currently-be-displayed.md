When you drag an image to a word doc, sometimes it can not be displayed. No other online answers solved this problem. But 

Two solutions: 
1\. You could click insert -> Picture -> Picture From File and choose the picture you want to insert 

2\. You could drag your picture onto a powerpoint first, then copy paste it onto your word doc
