Dave's Answer:

As with much to do with the iPhone, there's a bit of a trick to getting this working smoothly so you can have control over your [iPhone music](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html), while keeping it as darn easy to work with as possible. The answer? **Create a [custom playlist](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html) for your iPhone music library**.

Let me show you how to do it - it's pretty darn easy.

Pop into iTunes and choose **[File](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html)** --> **New Playlist**. Now rename it to something logical: I use "iPhone Sync":

![Apple iTunes in Mac OS X: New Playlist for iPhone](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/0-blog-pics/itunes-playlists-iphone.png)

Now you can just drag and drop music from your [iTunes library](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html) (in iTunes, needless to say) into this new playlist. When you're ready, you'll see a bunch of music happily ensconced in the playlist (remember, it's all just "pointers": the actual music files are still safely in the main music library, so if you delete something from a playlist , you haven't deleted the original music file itself):

![Apple iTunes in Mac OS X: New Playlist for iPhone](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/0-blog-pics/itunes-iphone-sync-playlist.png)

Notice here on the very bottom of the iTunes [window](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html) it shows you how many songs are in the playlist, along with the size and [total playing time](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html). For my current playlist, it's 140 songs, 10.1 hours, for a total [file size](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html) of 851MB, no problem for my 4GB Apple iPhone.

At this point, you need to plug in your iPhone if you haven't already done so. It'll pop up the usual summary screen:

[![Apple iPhone: Summary view in iTunes](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/0-blog-pics/itunes-iphone-setup-summary.png)](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/0-blog-pics/itunes-iphone-setup-summary.png)

Cool. Now click on the "Music" tab and you'll see that you can select "sync playlist". Do that, then select the new [iPhone sync](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html) playlist you've created, as shown here:

![Apple iTunes in Mac OS X: Synchronizing New Playlist with iPhone](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/0-blog-pics/itunes-iphone-sync-music.png)

Set things up just as I have shown and that's all there is to it. Now click "Sync" in iTunes and it'll synchronize your playlist with the iPhone music library and next time you click on that ole' "iPod" button on the iPhone, your selections from you music library will be present.

Oh, and one really nice feature of creating this playlist is that you can manage it, adding and [deleting](http://www.askdavetaylor.com/copy_itunes_music_onto_apple_iphone.html) songs to your heart's desire, even without your iPhone present. Whatever changes you make are instantly applied to your iPhone music library the very next time you synchronize the device. Suddenly even 8GB might feel small. :-)
