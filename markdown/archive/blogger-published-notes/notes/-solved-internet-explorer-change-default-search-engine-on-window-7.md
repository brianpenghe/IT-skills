at the bar where you type in www address, you will see a little triangle , click that 
You will see a menu where the frequently visited websites are shown. At the bottom right of that menu, click the "add" 
Then you will be directed to the website of ie add-ons and you can choose to install, say, google as your default engine. 
Without needing to restart, you will see the modifications applied after you finish installation.
