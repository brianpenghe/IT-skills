"1. Click on the link at the bottom of each message ([https://lists.caltech.edu/listinfo/marketplace](https://lists.caltech.edu/listinfo/marketplace)). On the bottom of that page, there is a place to fill in your email to unsubscribe. **Note: Due to Caltech's recent IT changes, you can only do this while on the Caltech network (or via VPN).**

2\. Using the email address that is subscribed to this list, send an email to[marketplace-leave@caltech.edu](mailto:marketplace-leave@caltech.edu). It doesn't matter what you put in the subject line or body of the email (you can leave it blank). **Use this option if you are away from Caltech and have no way of getting onto the Caltech network.**
