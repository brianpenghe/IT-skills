For windows version, follow this instruction: 
[Outlook Office 2010, 2013, 2016](https://www.alumni.caltech.edu/s/Setting-up-Office-365-on-Outlook.pdf) (PDF Instructions) 

For Mac version, please read: 
1\. Open Microsoft outlook on your Mac 
2\. On the top left, Outlook -> preferences 
3\. In the pop-up window, Accounts 
4\. In the accounts window, press "+" on bottom left -> New account 
5\. type your alumni addrew, probably Firstnamelastname@alumni.caltech.edu 
6\. a caltech log in window will pop up. Please CLOSE IT!!! 
7\. You will go back to a window saying "connecting to Office 365... Authentication couldn't be completed". Please click "not office 365?" on top right 
8\. In the new window, click IMAP/POP 
9\. In the manual set up window, choose IMAP for type, 
     Firstnamelastname@alumni.caltech.edu as email address, 
     Firstnamelastname@alumni.caltech.onmicrosoft.com for username 
     your password 
    outlook.office365.com for incoming mail server 
    smtp.office365.com for outgoing server 
    click Add account 
10\. Click Done
