I'm posting this not because the answer is hard to find, but because the official online answer is not quite right due to browser issues. 

1. Go to [Manage your Kindle Unlimited Membership](https://www.amazon.com/gp/kindle/ku/ku_central).
2. [](https://www.blogger.com/null)Under Manage Membership select Cancel Kindle Unlimited Membership.
3. [](https://www.blogger.com/null)Select End Membership on \[DATE\].

You would get stuck at Step 2 because the page you open will be blank.

The solution: use a cell phone, e.g. iphone safari browser works on that page.
