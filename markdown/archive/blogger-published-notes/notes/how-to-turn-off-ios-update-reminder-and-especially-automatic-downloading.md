Settings -> General -> Storage & iCloud Usage -> Manage Storage -> tap on the ios update you want to skip and select delete 

However, Apple will still try to find time to bring new ios update back to your phone again. 
In order to stop them, you have to fill you phone with music or photos and leave less space than enough for the new update, so that they won't download any more.reference: 
http://osxdaily.com/2016/01/04/stop-ios-software-update-notification/
