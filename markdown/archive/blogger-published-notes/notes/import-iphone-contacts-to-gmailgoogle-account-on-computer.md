1\. log on iCloud using your computer 
2\. Go to contacts and select all of them 
3\. click the gear sign on the bottom left and export vCard and save 
4\. log onto google contacts (from google voice or gmail or calendar) 
5\. more -> import -> upload the contacts 
6\. Now go to your google voice/gmail, you will see the contacts updated
