Registering a new account every three months and follow your previous account's lists doesn't sound so hard if you keep track. But what if Spotify has started tracking your behavior? 

That's what happened to my account. But their tracking system only tracks your credit card information. 

So the solution is : use a different card to pay the $0.99 when you use another account and enjoy cheap music!
