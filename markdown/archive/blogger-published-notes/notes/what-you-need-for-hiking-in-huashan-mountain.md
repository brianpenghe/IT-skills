1\. What to take with you: 
gloves: for grabbing the chains for steep stairs climbing 
napkin/tissues: because their toilets don't have that 
money: to buy food/water on your way up/down.  The prices are not low but reasonable 
Your ID and other hiking necessities like: 
water, 
food, 
clothes, 
earplug and eyecover for sleeping, 
a mask to protect you from smokers, 
a camera and selfie stick, 
handsoap for post-hike clean up(because you have to use your hands to do this hiking), 
cell phone, 
mosquito repellent, 
first-aid stuff, 
etc. 

2\. Where to live on the mountain: 
There are multiple hotels there, you could book the hotels online like 携程 etc. The hotels usually provide hot water, which is a nice thing 

3\. How to get to Huashan: 
I chose Subway + 高铁railway/train 
You could book 高铁railway/train ticket from Xi'an Bei西安北 to Huashan Bei华山北 online or at the train station. 
Xi'an Subway can take you to Xi'an Bei西安北station. 

4\. How to get to the mountain: 
There are free minibus No.1 and No.2  . I took the No.1 . They can drive you to the visitor center of the mountain. Never trust any local drivers who aggressively try to persuade you give you a ride, which is usually expensive. 

5\. At the visiter center, you can decide whether to take the bus + cable car up the hill or hike. It's up to you. But you have to know that the nearst hotel on top is 6-hr away from the foot if you hike up. So be prepared.
