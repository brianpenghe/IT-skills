1\. open that page and copy the link 
2\. open wechat and press moments 
3\. press and hold the camera button on top right and paste the page link 
4\. send to moments 
5\. click on the link you just pasted, the page will be opened by wechat 
6\. click on the three dots on top right, press "share on moments" 
7\. delete the link you posted in wechat moments 

That's it!
