In genetics, **floxing** refers to the sandwiching of a [DNA](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA "DNA") sequence (which is then said to be **floxed**) between two _[lox P](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cre-Lox_recombination "Cre-Lox recombination")_ sites. The terms are constructed upon the phrase "flanking/flanked by LoxP". Recombination between LoxP sites is catalysed by [Cre recombinase](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cre_recombinase "Cre recombinase"). Floxing a gene allows it to be deleted (knocked out), translocated or inverted in a process called [Cre-Lox recombination](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cre-Lox_recombination "Cre-Lox recombination"). [\[1\]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floxing#cite_note-1)
