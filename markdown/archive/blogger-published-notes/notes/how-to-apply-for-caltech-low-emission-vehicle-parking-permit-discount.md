The discount is 20% off 
1\. check whether your vehicle is qualified [here](https://parking.caltech.edu/documents/45-qualified_list_for_web_-_2016.pdf) 
2\. print out this [form](https://www.parking.caltech.edu/documents/36-low_emission_vehicle_form.pdf) 
3\. complete the form and submit to the parking office on Wilson 

Note: If you haven't got a permit yet, you may apply for one
