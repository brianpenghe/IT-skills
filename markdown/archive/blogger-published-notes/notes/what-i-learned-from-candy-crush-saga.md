1\. Don't always get jealous of others' luck. They are either more hard-working, smarter or richer than you. 

[![](http://2.bp.blogspot.com/-zE6pWWfzf08/VplwUaAlnDI/AAAAAAAAIgU/nli9ExQeBTo/s1600/p.jpg)](http://2.bp.blogspot.com/-zE6pWWfzf08/VplwUaAlnDI/AAAAAAAAIgU/nli9ExQeBTo/s1600/p.jpg) 

2\. Quit immediately when you know you are not gonna win. Getting rid of time sinks enables you to play more meaningful games. 

[![](http://4.bp.blogspot.com/-Of7iF_765xA/VplwcCMoH5I/AAAAAAAAIgg/s9IyMMZ7LPs/s1600/p%2B%25282%2529.jpg)](http://4.bp.blogspot.com/-Of7iF_765xA/VplwcCMoH5I/AAAAAAAAIgg/s9IyMMZ7LPs/s1600/p%2B%25282%2529.jpg) 

3\. Never blindly believe people's compliments. They distort your self-reflection  

[![](http://1.bp.blogspot.com/-CM31qaFrrAQ/VplwiiX3FKI/AAAAAAAAIgs/-D-_ofqlEn8/s400/p%2B%25282%2529.jpg)](http://1.bp.blogspot.com/-CM31qaFrrAQ/VplwiiX3FKI/AAAAAAAAIgs/-D-_ofqlEn8/s1600/p%2B%25282%2529.jpg) 

4\. Sometimes you can't quit something or someone, not because you love it so much, but because you are just used to playing with them.
