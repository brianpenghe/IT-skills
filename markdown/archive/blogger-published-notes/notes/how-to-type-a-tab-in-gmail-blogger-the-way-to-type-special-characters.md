To type a tab: 
hold alt and type 009 on the number pad 

For other characters you can refer to this page http://www.wikihow.com/Type-Symbols-Using-the-ALT-Key 

I've copied them here 

# # # Method 1 of 5: On a PC

1. [![Type Symbols Using the ALT Key Step 1 Version 2.jpg](http://pad3.whstatic.com/images/thumb/7/7f/Type-Symbols-Using-the-ALT-Key-Step-1-Version-2.jpg/670px-Type-Symbols-Using-the-ALT-Key-Step-1-Version-2.jpg)](http://www.wikihow.com/Image:Type-Symbols-Using-the-ALT-Key-Step-1-Version-2.jpg)

 1

 **Learn how to key in an Alt-code.**Hold down the Alt key and put in the numbers on the keypad to the right of the keyboard. When you finish entering the numbers and release the Alt key, the symbol will appear.

2. [![Type Symbols Using the ALT Key Step 2 Version 2.jpg](http://pad2.whstatic.com/images/thumb/3/35/Type-Symbols-Using-the-ALT-Key-Step-2-Version-2.jpg/670px-Type-Symbols-Using-the-ALT-Key-Step-2-Version-2.jpg)](http://www.wikihow.com/Image:Type-Symbols-Using-the-ALT-Key-Step-2-Version-2.jpg)

 2

 **Learn how to key in Alt codes on a laptop.** Press the "Num Lock" key. (If it's blue or grey, hold down the Fn key before pressing it.) The Number Lock activated the gray or blue keys on your keyboard; they should be arranged in a square. Hold down the ALT key, hit the & then the # and then use these numbers.
