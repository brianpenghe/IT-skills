0\. I highly recommend you watch [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=koMiaSuljxE), although it's not perfect 
1\. I bought [this screen repair kit ](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00L9RWWCC/ref=oh_aui_detailpage_o01_s00?ie=UTF8&psc=1) 
2\. Don't lock your broken iphone 5c. Let the battery die over time 
3\. Take out the two screws here: 

[![](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/-O2TlLSIjhe4/V9OOznecLzI/AAAAAAAAJkw/LuXQdvK6Xl830NFvlBa08Vnv1hlGd5SqgCLcB/s320/2016-09-09%2B14.10.02.jpg)](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/-O2TlLSIjhe4/V9OOznecLzI/AAAAAAAAJkw/LuXQdvK6Xl830NFvlBa08Vnv1hlGd5SqgCLcB/s1600/2016-09-09%2B14.10.02.jpg)

4\. Use a tiny screw driver to insert in to the power hole and lift up the screen a little bit. 

[![](https://2.bp.blogspot.com/-yMFdWpfQxwQ/V9OPhPs52sI/AAAAAAAAJk0/fqCsfIFUwhcFHQ1zj4k6ocCvaqQqJaFvwCLcB/s320/2016-09-09%2B14.17.15.jpg)](https://2.bp.blogspot.com/-yMFdWpfQxwQ/V9OPhPs52sI/AAAAAAAAJk0/fqCsfIFUwhcFHQ1zj4k6ocCvaqQqJaFvwCLcB/s1600/2016-09-09%2B14.17.15.jpg)

 5\. so that you could insert your guitar pick 

[![](https://2.bp.blogspot.com/-U_IYj1V3dro/V9OPkFcwBPI/AAAAAAAAJlA/4yXcuinfbsIeogWuSrZdUjOYEQCrmiByACLcB/s320/2016-09-09%2B14.17.51.jpg)](https://2.bp.blogspot.com/-U_IYj1V3dro/V9OPkFcwBPI/AAAAAAAAJlA/4yXcuinfbsIeogWuSrZdUjOYEQCrmiByACLcB/s1600/2016-09-09%2B14.17.51.jpg)

6\. and open the front plate 

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-zd8NOCVaH00/V9OPkHQmIUI/AAAAAAAAJk4/FpxySLYnLXchoOezurAeffqi9_X2C5V1QCEw/s320/2016-09-09%2B14.18.58.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-zd8NOCVaH00/V9OPkHQmIUI/AAAAAAAAJk4/FpxySLYnLXchoOezurAeffqi9_X2C5V1QCEw/s1600/2016-09-09%2B14.18.58.jpg)

7\. Now, you need to take off the screws holding the top right metal and take out the top right metal 
8\. Use the blue tool to unplug the front plate 

[![](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-_5PMl4ZOn_w/V9OPkKKPbeI/AAAAAAAAJk8/rXTtJ9LxAPYLavQI56RYly5ufpp_-33OQCEw/s320/2016-09-09%2B14.26.01.jpg)](https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-_5PMl4ZOn_w/V9OPkKKPbeI/AAAAAAAAJk8/rXTtJ9LxAPYLavQI56RYly5ufpp_-33OQCEw/s1600/2016-09-09%2B14.26.01.jpg)

9.Now you've taken the broken screen off. 

[![](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/--H7Mn_NrFug/V9OPkqUt9wI/AAAAAAAAJlE/RQf6uYJItucl_uzxCCXmWMe0JaSUSve4gCEw/s320/2016-09-09%2B14.27.01.jpg)](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/--H7Mn_NrFug/V9OPkqUt9wI/AAAAAAAAJlE/RQf6uYJItucl_uzxCCXmWMe0JaSUSve4gCEw/s1600/2016-09-09%2B14.27.01.jpg)

10\. put the new one back following the reverse order of this protocol.
