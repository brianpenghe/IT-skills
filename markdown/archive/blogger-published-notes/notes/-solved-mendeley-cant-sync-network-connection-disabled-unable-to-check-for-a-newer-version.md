There are two errors for my Mendeley: 
1\. It can't sync. Always shows error messages about unable to upload to mendeley.com whenever I click sync 
2\. Can't update to a newer version when I click help -> update 

The solution: 

1) Open Mendeley Desktop. 
2) Click Ctrl + Shift + D. 
3) Go to the Settings tab. 
4) Set General\_IgnoreSslErrors to ‘True’. 
5) Restart Mendeley Desktop. 

reference: http://support.mendeley.com/customer/portal/questions/16335126-unable-to-check-for-a-newer-version
