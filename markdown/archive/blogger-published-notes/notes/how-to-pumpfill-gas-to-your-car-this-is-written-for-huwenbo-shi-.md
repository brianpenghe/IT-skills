Step 0: Check your tank's position and park in the gas station so that your tank is facing the pumping machine 

Step 1: There should be a switch around Driver's seat to open the door of the gas tank. You should park well, shut off your engine and open the tank door. 

Step 2 : Make a payment. If you use credit card/debit card/Vons card, go to Step2a. If you pay by cash, go to Step 2b 
Step 2a: Insert your credit card/debit card, enter your zip code 
Step 2b: Go to the Staff at the station and make cash purchase of whatever amount/price you want to fill 

Step 3: Remove the nozzle 
Step 4: Select Grade/type of gasoline you want 

Step 5: Insert the nozzle into your tank opening and press down the handle to start fueling 
Step 6: Watch the price/amount increasing until you think it's enough; Or you can make use of the threshold controller on the handle to let it automatically stop when full. 

Step 7: Close your tank door. 
Step 8: Put back the nozzle and select receipt or no receipt 
Step 9: You are good to go.
