1. In the top-right corner of the browser window, click the Chrome menu ![Chrome menu](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/4003BF51063E6DC3E65713AE5B50EE797EE6 "Chrome menu").
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Click **Show advanced settings**.
4. Under "Privacy," click the **Content settings** button.
5. Under "Pop-ups," select **Allow all sites to show pop-ups**.
