This quick tutorial shows how we enable different colors to be revealed to distinguish different file types etc on your Mac Terminal. 
1\. nano ~/.bash\_profile 
    or using any other texteditor to open it 
2\. add a line into that file: 
export CLICOLOR=1 
3\. save and restart the terminal 

reference: http://www.marinamele.com/2014/05/customize-colors-of-your-terminal-in-mac-os-x.html
