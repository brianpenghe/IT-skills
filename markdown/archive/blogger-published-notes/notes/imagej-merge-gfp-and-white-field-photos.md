Drag the two images to ImageJ to open; 
Image -> color -> merge channels 
Add GFP to green and white to gray. 

Ctrl + shift + Z and Ctrl + shift+C to further adjust 

One trick:  For GFP signal, you'd better use G4.5 method of scaling to make the weak signals visible
