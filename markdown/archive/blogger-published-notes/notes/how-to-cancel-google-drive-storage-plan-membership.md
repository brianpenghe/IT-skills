The old protocol no long works because Google drive has been migrated to Google One. 

Therefore, what you should do is: 

1. On your computer, go to [Google One](https://one.google.com/).
2. At the top, click Settings ![Settings](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/glqypaaGcfXA8StlcFQgpr_2_59Wnq0b5IVnhMDUSozcS9_67to_ur9SkLIAqEOM5bY3=w18-h18 "Settings") ![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/nHFGZ_9xjCh-mP83zMzXQVJF5VYf2n6kwoBIxB2zv3V4VPT4gNTtBye8lYznogLqLPY=w13-h18 "and then") Cancel membership.
3. In Google Play, next to "Google One," click Manage ![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/nHFGZ_9xjCh-mP83zMzXQVJF5VYf2n6kwoBIxB2zv3V4VPT4gNTtBye8lYznogLqLPY=w13-h18 "and then") Cancel subscription.
4. Follow the steps on the screen.
