* 1

 Click the "Tools" menu button. If the menu bar is not visible, press the "alt" button to make it appear.

* 2

 Click the "Manage Add-ons" option in the "Tools" menu. This brings up a new dialog box.

3 click search providers 

4 at the bottom click "find more providers", then at the microsoft website you can install google search provider 

5 go back to you manage add-on menu, click on the bing, and click "remove"
