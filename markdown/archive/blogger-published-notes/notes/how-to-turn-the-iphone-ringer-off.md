To turn the iPhone ringer off and use iPhone silent mode, all you need to do is flip a switch.

On the [upper left-hand side of the iPhone](http://ipod.about.com/od/introductiontotheiphone/ss/Anatomy-Of-Iphone-4.htm) is a small switch. It’s just above the volume control and below the phone's corner. This is the iPhone ringer.
