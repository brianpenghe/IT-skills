If you see someone sharing a personal status with either prizes won / scientific article published etc, you may want to know where that button is, coz you only see an "update status" button next to the blank box. 

Facebook, however, provides a lot of choices for your action. 

The trick: Click on your small profile picture on top right to go to your profile page 
Then you will see three options: update a status, upload photo/video, or add a life event. 
Let's choose the life event. And then the hidden options will show up! 

Give it a try!
