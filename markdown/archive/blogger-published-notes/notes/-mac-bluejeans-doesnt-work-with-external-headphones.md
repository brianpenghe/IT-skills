If may occur that your bluejeans doesn't like your external headphone connected to your mac 
You may try System Preferences  -> Sound -> Output -> External headphones but it still didn't work. 

The solution is in Bluejeans itself 

You should do this: 
 BlueJeans -> Preferences -> Devices -> Speaker -> Headphones 

That will do the work
