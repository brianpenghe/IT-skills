When I wanted to check the photos I took earlier, I couldn't find them. 
This happened today and I searched online for solutions. 
Some suggest settings -> privacy -> photo 
some suggest settings -> general -> restricition. 

Neither worked. 

My solution: 
1\. restart your phone 
2\. take a picture, and view that picture. 
3\. Wait a little bit, the gray buttons for sharing will turn blue. Then all my previous "unsaved" photos showed up. 

I hope Apple can fix this bug.
