# Instagram Account Management Skills

## Purpose

This guide summarizes practical workflows for: - Archiving Instagram
content - Understanding archive behavior - Managing Stories and
Highlights - Deciding between an existing account and a new professional
account - Creating a second Instagram account

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Archive vs Delete

## Archive

-   Removes the content from public view.
-   Only you can see archived items.
-   Likes, comments, captions, and posting date are preserved.
-   You can restore the content later.

## Delete

-   Permanently removes the content.
-   Likes, comments, and engagement are lost.
-   Cannot normally be recovered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Bulk Archiving Posts

Mobile app:

Profile → ☰ → Your Activity → Photos and Videos → Posts → Select →
Archive

Notes: - Multiple posts can be archived together. - Photos and carousel
posts generally support bulk archiving.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Reels Limitations

Current Instagram behavior is inconsistent.

Observed behavior: - Many accounts only see **Delete** for bulk-selected
Reels. - Some individual Reels have an Archive option. - Others only
provide Delete.

There is currently no reliable bulk archive workflow for Reels across
all accounts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Story Archive

Story Archive is different from post archive.

When enabled: - Stories automatically move to Story Archive after 24
hours. - Only you can see Story Archive. - Story Archive is separate
from Highlights.

Highlights: - Visible on your profile. - Removing a Highlight does NOT
remove the Story from Story Archive.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Private Account vs Archive vs Close Friends

Private account - Existing posts visible to approved followers.

Archive - Existing posts visible only to you.

Close Friends - Primarily intended for sharing selected new content. -
Existing public posts generally cannot simply be converted to Close
Friends.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Existing Account vs New Professional Account

## Existing account

Advantages - Keep followers. - Keep engagement history. - Maintain
username.

Disadvantages - Requires cleanup. - Mixed personal/professional
identity. - Reels are difficult to archive in bulk.

## New professional account

Advantages - Clean branding. - Consistent niche. - No cleanup
required. - Easier profile optimization.

Disadvantages - Must build audience from scratch.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Creator vs Business Account

Both are Professional accounts.

Creator - Better suited for individuals building a personal brand. -
Appropriate for researchers, educators, founders, creators.

Business - Better suited for companies and organizations.

A Creator account can later be switched to Business if needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Creating a Second Instagram Account

Using the Instagram app:

1.  Open Instagram.
2.  Go to Profile.
3.  Tap your username at the top.
4.  Tap Add Instagram Account.
5.  Tap Create New Account.
6.  Choose username.
7.  Verify email or phone.
8.  Finish setup.

Instagram allows multiple logged-in accounts (up to five) and easy
switching from the profile page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Practical Recommendation

If your current account is primarily: - personal life - travel - food -
miscellaneous memories

and your new goal is: - entrepreneurship - research - AI - business -
professional education

then creating a separate professional account is often simpler than
cleaning up years of unrelated content.

A dedicated professional profile provides: - consistent branding -
easier follower expectations - less maintenance - freedom to keep
personal memories on your original account.
